The instrument

What a force plate measures

A force plate measures the forces between a person’s feet and the ground during standing, walking, running or jumping.

Researchers and coaches use these measurements to study balance, movement and athletic performance. But current accurate model comes at a steep price.

$30,000 The price of a current accurate force plate

The design problem

Reducing the cost of a force plate

The original force plate design

Five expensive sensors, one at the center and one at each corner, contributed to the high cost of the design.

My approach

I wanted to cut sensor costs by keeping one multi-axis sensor at the center and using cheaper load sensors around the edges.

A hybrid sensor architecture

Only one multi-axis sensor at the center to cut the cost

Lower-cost load sensors near the plate’s edges

I wanted to reserve the more expensive sensor for the center and use cheaper sensors elsewhere. I needed to test whether this arrangement could still collect useful force data.

KAIST · Summer after Grade 11

Testing the idea before manufacture

Professor Yong-Jin Yoon supervised my work at KAIST’s Just Do It Laboratory. I used finite element method (FEM) simulations to examine my proposed plate.

The simulations let me examine the design before starting the physical build.

Comparative FEM study

Comparing three force plate designs

  1. A design built for high precision
  2. A simpler, less expensive design
  3. My combined sensor design

With the professor’s guidance, I helped prepare the CAD models and set their materials, applied forces and supports. I also worked out which settings needed to stay the same across the designs.

Simulation workflow

Working through the simulation results

A simulation took several hours at times. Once it finished, I reviewed the output before deciding what to change for the next run.

I checked how the settings I had chosen affected the predicted behavior. If the output differed from what I expected, I went back to the model and adjusted it until the expected results came out.

Simulation findings

What I learned from the simulations

In the simulations, my design offered a compromise between the two comparison models. The results suggested that the proposed hybrid structure was worth testing as an actual physical prototype plate.

I wrote a research paper based on the simulation study. I then began making a prototype to examine how the design would behave outside the simulation.

I still needed measurements from actual hardware to assess accuracy.
